# Portfolio-Project

Technologies inside your website

Tailwind CSS: controls the layout and styling, including the phone, tablet and desktop versions.

GSAP and ScrollTrigger: run the animations, such as the name sliding in, items fading in as you scroll, counters, the timeline line and the progress bar.

Google Fonts: Cormorant Garamond for headings, Inter for body text and Homemade Apple for the handwritten tagline.

Inline SVG icons: the laptop, calculator, folders, globe and coins are drawn in the page itself, so they need no image files.

JavaScript: powers the mobile menu, the highlighted menu link as you scroll, and the contact form.
What the website does

Navigation: a menu that stays at the top, with a hamburger menu on phones and tablets.

Hero section: your name, title, summary and buttons, with space for your portrait.

About and Skills: your profile, three headline stats and a skills card.

Experience: a timeline of your five roles from 2017 to the present.
Education, Certification and References: your degree, the Agentic AI certificate and your two character references.

Contact: buttons for email, phone and LinkedIn, plus the message form that opens the visitor’s email app with the message ready.

Responsive layout: adjusts for phones, tablets and desktops.

Accessibility: tap-friendly buttons, keyboard focus outlines and animations that switch off for people who prefer reduced motion.
